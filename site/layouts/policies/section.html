Official policies

Ready-made rules you include in your project's .stonewall.yml. Included policies apply first, your own rules last.

Add one with stonewall policy include <url>, or run stonewall policy pick to tick the ones you need from this list. Remote policies are reviewed once and cached; stonewall policy update fetches new versions.

These are official stonewall.sh policies and should not be changed locally. Always review remote policies before applying them. Stonewall and its policies come without warranty of any kind.

Every policy file follows the policy schema. stonewall policy validate <url|path> checks a file or URL against it.
